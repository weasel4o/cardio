About this model

What is real and what is simulated

Real, from public reports: congenital aortic valve disease (widely reported as bicuspid); Ross procedure on 16 April 1997; pulmonary valve replacement in March 2018 at Cedars-Sinai, where a planned catheter procedure became open-heart surgery; new aortic valve at Cleveland Clinic in October 2020; pacemaker in March 2024, which he attributed to scar tissue from earlier surgeries.

Illustrative: the heart geometry is procedural, not reconstructed from his imaging. Valve phenotype (R–L fusion with raphe), prosthesis types, the dual-chamber pacing system and the conduction disturbance before 2024 are shown for teaching.

Simulated: every number in the telemetry, ECG and trend chart follows a representative natural history of bicuspid valve disease and Ross conduit degeneration. None are his measurements. Thresholds follow ACC/AHA and ESC severe-disease criteria (Vmax ≥ 4 m/s, mean gradient ≥ 40 mmHg, AVA ≤ 1.0 cm²; RVOT peak gradient > 64 mmHg).
